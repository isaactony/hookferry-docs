title: "Retries and replay"
description: "When Hookferry retries a failed delivery automatically, the full retry schedule for HTTPS destinations, and how replay differs from a retry."
---

Hookferry delivers an event again in two ways. A **retry** happens automatically after a failed delivery attempt. A **replay** happens only when you ask for one.

## What counts as a failed attempt

A delivery attempt fails if your endpoint:

- Returns any status code outside `200`–`299`, including `3xx` redirects.
- Doesn't respond within 30 seconds.
- Refuses the connection or can't be reached.

## Retry schedule for HTTPS destinations

HTTPS destinations get up to 8 delivery attempts: the first attempt plus 7 retries. Times are measured from the previous attempt.

| Attempt | Delay after previous attempt |
| :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Immediately |
| 2 | 1 minute |
| 3 | 5 minutes |
| 4 | 30 minutes |
| 5 | 1 hour |
| 6 | 3 hours |
| 7 | 6 hours |
| 8 | 12 hours |

The last attempt happens about 22 hours and 36 minutes after the first. If attempt 8 fails, the event status becomes `failed` and Hookferry stops retrying.

## CLI destinations aren't retried

A CLI destination gets one attempt per event:

- If no `hookferry listen` session is connected, the event becomes `undelivered`.
- If the session is connected but your local app fails, the event becomes `failed`.

Local apps restart often during development, so automatic retries would send old events at unexpected times. Replay them when you're ready instead.

## Replay sends a stored event again

A replay delivers a stored event again, using the original body and headers. You can replay any event inside your plan's retention period, whatever its status.

A replay:

- Goes to every active route on the source, or to one route you choose.
- Gets a new `Hookferry-Signature` and the header `Hookferry-Replay: true`.
- Creates new delivery attempts. It doesn't change the original attempts.
- Is not retried automatically, even for HTTPS destinations.

To replay events, see [Replay failed events](/guides/replay-failed-events).

## Make your handler safe to run twice

Retries and replays mean your app can receive the same event more than once. Store the `Hookferry-Event-Id` header value after you process an event, and skip events you've already processed.
